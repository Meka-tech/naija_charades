## Description (4,000 max)
Bring the gist, the jollof and the vibes. Naija Charades is the party game that turns any hangout, family gathering or Detty December night into pure fun.

HOW IT WORKS
Hold your phone up to your forehead. Your squad acts out, describes or sings the word on screen, and you try to guess it before the timer runs out. Tilt down when you get it right and tilt up to pass. Prefer to act? Switch to "Others Guess" and let your friends do the guessing.

1,800+ CARDS, 17 CATEGORIES
- Nollywood: from old-school legends to today's box-office stars
- Music: Afrobeats bangers and global hits to sing or hum
- Naija Slang (NEW): Japa, Sapa, Wahala, Detty December and more
- Naija Places (NEW): cities, landmarks and hotspots across Nigeria
- Food: jollof, suya, puff puff, Nkwobi and every party favourite
- Popular Personalities, Movies/Series, Sports, Brands, Cartoons, Anime & Manga, Heroes & Villains, Games, Animals, Occupation, Accents & Impressions and Act it Out

PLAY YOUR WAY
- Quick Play: pick a category and start a round in seconds
- Versus: split into teams, choose your rounds and battle for bragging rights
- Favourites: save the categories your crew loves most
- Fresh content: new cards and categories arrive without an app update

MADE FOR NAIJA
Every card is picked with a Nigerian twist, so the references land with your friends, cousins and aunties. Clean and family-friendly, it works for game night, birthdays, road trips, owambe after-parties and holiday hangouts.

No sign-up needed. Download now and find out who in your squad is the real charades champion!
